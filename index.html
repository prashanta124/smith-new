Profile

Scholar of media industries, labour, and platform power.

Dr Smith Mehta is Assistant Professor in Media Studies at Rijksuniversiteit Groningen. His work examines how digital platforms, policy shifts, production cultures, and creative labour reshape audiovisual industries, with particular attention to India and Global South media contexts.

His academic trajectory combines doctoral research at Queensland University of Technology, industry experience at Viacom18 and Voot, and public-facing scholarship through op-eds, podcasts, talks, and media interviews.

Research themes

Four connected areas

01

Digital screen industries

Digital transformation of Indian and Global South audiovisual industries, from streaming platforms to new production ecologies.

02

Platform cultures

Creator economies, intermediaries, content negotiation, informal labour, and the shifting relationship between platforms and cultural work.

03

Policy, power, censorship

Media governance, digital censorship, regulation, public policy, and the political economy of screen content.

04

Gendered production cultures

Gender, precarity, representation, and the workplace politics of digital production cultures after #MeToo.
